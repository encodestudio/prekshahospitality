📜 General Booking Policy

Please read carefully before you arrive — these terms apply to your reservation.

🏨 Reservation Confirmation

Bookings are subject to availability and are confirmed only on receipt of the required advance payment. Group bookings may need a higher advance.

🕐 Check-in / Check-out

Check-in: 12:00 PM  |  Check-out: 11:00 AM. Early check-in / late check-out are subject to availability and may attract extra charges.

🆔 Valid ID Required

Every guest must present a valid Government-issued photo ID at check-in (Aadhaar, Passport, Driving Licence, Voter ID, etc.). Foreign nationals must produce a valid Passport and Visa.

👫 Guest Eligibility

Unmarried couples are not permitted to stay. Guests carrying local ID proof (Ayodhya/Faizabad or nearby addresses) will not be permitted to check in. Management reserves the right to refuse admission for non-compliant bookings.

💳 Payment

Advance payment confirms the reservation; the balance is payable at check-in unless agreed otherwise in writing. Payments accepted via UPI, bank transfer, card, or cash.

📝 Cancellation, Amendment & No-Show

Cancellation/amendment charges apply as per your booking confirmation (separate terms may apply for peak season, festivals, and group bookings). Refunds are processed to the original payment mode. Not arriving on your check-in date without prior intimation may result in cancellation and forfeiture of the advance paid.

🧒 Children & Extra Bed

Children below 5 years sharing existing bedding stay complimentary. Extra mattress/bed is available on request and is chargeable as per the prevailing tariff.

🍽️ Food, Smoking, Alcohol & Pets

Non-vegetarian food, smoking, and alcoholic beverages are strictly prohibited anywhere on the premises; violations may lead to immediate cancellation of stay without refund. Pets are not permitted.

🙏 Guest Conduct & Liability

Guests must maintain a peaceful, respectful environment; damage to hotel property is chargeable to the guest. Please keep valuables secure — the hotel is not responsible for loss, theft, or damage to personal belongings except as required by law.

⚖️ Force Majeure & Jurisdiction

The hotel is not liable for disruptions arising from events beyond its reasonable control (natural calamities, government restrictions, pandemics, strikes, etc.). All disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh.
