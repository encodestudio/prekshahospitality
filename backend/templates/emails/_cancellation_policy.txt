CANCELLATION & REFUND POLICY — applies to all bookings unless otherwise agreed in writing
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Individual Bookings (FIT): >7 days before check-in: no charges, advance refunded (minus bank/gateway charges). 3-7 days before: 50% of booking value charged. <72 hours before, or No-Show: 100% of the first night's charges forfeited.
- Group Bookings (10+ Rooms): >30 days before arrival: no charges. 15-30 days: 25% of total booking value. 7-14 days: 50% of total booking value. <7 days, or No-Show: 100% of the advance forfeited.
- Festival & Peak Season Bookings: Bookings during festivals, long weekends, New Year, Ram Navami, Deepotsav, Kartik Purnima, and other notified peak periods may be non-cancellable, non-refundable, and non-amendable unless stated otherwise in your booking confirmation.
- Amendments: Changes to arrival/departure dates, number of rooms, or guest details are subject to availability and prevailing tariff. Amendments within 72 hours of arrival may be treated as a cancellation and fresh booking.
- Early Departure & No-Show: Checking out before the confirmed departure date may still be charged for the unused room nights. Not arriving on the scheduled check-in date without prior written intimation is a No-Show - cancellation charges apply and the room may be released for sale.
- Refunds: Eligible refunds are processed within 7-10 working days of cancellation approval, to the original mode of payment only. Bank/payment gateway charges, taxes, or other third-party transaction charges may be deducted.
- Force Majeure & Management's Decision: The hotel is not liable for cancellations/delays due to events beyond its reasonable control (natural disasters, government restrictions, strikes, pandemics, etc.) - refunds/rescheduling in such cases are at management's discretion. The Management reserves the right to modify this policy without prior notice; exceptions are valid only if confirmed in writing.
