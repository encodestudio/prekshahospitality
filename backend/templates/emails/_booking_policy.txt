GENERAL BOOKING POLICY — please read before you arrive
---------------------------------------------------------
- Reservation Confirmation: Bookings are subject to availability and confirmed only on receipt of the required advance payment. Group bookings may need a higher advance.
- Check-in / Check-out: Check-in 12:00 PM, Check-out 11:00 AM. Early check-in / late check-out subject to availability, may attract extra charges.
- Valid ID Required: Every guest must present a valid Government-issued photo ID at check-in (Aadhaar, Passport, Driving Licence, Voter ID, etc.). Foreign nationals need a valid Passport and Visa.
- Guest Eligibility: Unmarried couples are not permitted to stay. Guests carrying local ID proof (Ayodhya/Faizabad or nearby addresses) will not be permitted to check in. Management reserves the right to refuse admission for non-compliant bookings.
- Payment: Advance payment confirms the reservation; balance payable at check-in unless agreed otherwise in writing. UPI, bank transfer, card, or cash accepted.
- Cancellation, Amendment & No-Show: Charges apply as per your booking confirmation (separate terms for peak season/festivals/group bookings). Refunds go to the original payment mode. Not arriving on the check-in date without prior intimation may result in cancellation and forfeiture of the advance.
- Children & Extra Bed: Children below 5 years sharing existing bedding stay complimentary. Extra mattress/bed available on request, chargeable as per tariff.
- Food, Smoking, Alcohol & Pets: Non-vegetarian food, smoking, and alcohol are strictly prohibited on the premises; violations may lead to immediate cancellation without refund. Pets are not permitted.
- Guest Conduct & Liability: Maintain a peaceful, respectful environment; damage to hotel property is chargeable to the guest. Keep valuables secure — the hotel is not responsible for loss/theft/damage except as required by law.
- Force Majeure & Jurisdiction: Hotel is not liable for disruptions beyond its reasonable control (natural calamities, government restrictions, pandemics, strikes, etc.). Disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh.
